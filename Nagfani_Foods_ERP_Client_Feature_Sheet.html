1. Executive Overview

Nagfani Foods ERP 3.0 is custom-engineered for traditional Rajasthani food manufacturers (Papad, Khakhra, Loung Sev, Bhujia, Pickles) operating multi-tier wholesale and distribution channels. The system replaces fragmented paper records, manual ledger reconciliation, and telephone order miscommunication with a unified, high-density digital workstation.

2. Complete Module Breakdown & Operator How-To Guide

Module 1: Sales & High-Speed Order Booking /v3/orders • /v3/orders/new

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Rapid 2-Pane Order Entry: Designed for rapid booking during morning order rush hours.
  • Customer Credit Intelligence: Real-time lookup of customer credit limits against current outstanding ledger balances with instant excess warnings.
  • Quick-Add Favorites Catalog: 1-click addition of top sellers (Urad Hari Mirch Papad, Rajasthani Loung Sev, Methi Khakhra, Ker Sangri Aachar).
  • Dynamic Quantity & Rate Modifiers: Directly editable quantity input fields with stepper buttons and line-item tax calculations.
  • Dual Operational Views: High-density data table and 4-stage Kanban Board (Received → Packed → Shipped → Delivered).
How to Execute an Order:
  1. Click "+ Create Order" or press F2.
  2. Select the party (e.g. Sharma General Store); observe instant credit standing and ledger balance updates.
  3. Assign delivery vehicle and delivery window (e.g. Van 01 Mukesh • Morning Run).
  4. Click items to add, type or step quantities; CGST, SGST, and consignment weight compute live.
  5. Click "Book Order Now"; the order is queued for warehouse packing, and a Tally sales voucher is created automatically.
Module 2: Fleet Dispatch & Route Logistics /v3/delivery

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Active Van Fleet Telemetry: Live monitoring of 4 dedicated Udaipur delivery vans (Van 01 Mukesh, Van 02 Sohan, Van 03 Ramesh, Van 04 Dinesh).
  • Payload Capacity Meters: Live weight tracking comparing consignment load (kg) against vehicle capacity to avoid overload fines.
  • Driver Stop Checklist: Sequential delivery stop manifests with customer name, sector beat, cash-to-collect, and tap-to-call phone buttons.
  • COD Cash Vault Tracking: Distinguishes cash physically inside van vaults from pending recoveries.
  • One-Click Run Sheet Printing: Print-optimized dispatch sheets with customer signature blocks.
How to Dispatch a Van:
  1. Open "Dispatch & Fleet" from the sidebar.
  2. Select a vehicle to view its assigned orders and delivery route stops.
  3. Click "Print Run Sheets" before drivers leave the godown.
  4. When a driver completes a delivery, tap "Delivered"; the status turns green and the cash collected is credited to the driver's vault balance.
Module 3: Food Manufacturing & Recipe BOM Batching /v3/production

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Pre-Set Recipe Library: Standardized Bill of Materials (BOM) for Urad Masala Papad, Loung Sev, and Methi Vacuum Khakhra.
  • Batch Multiplier Scaling: Scale batches by 1.0x, 1.5x, 2.0x, or 3.0x with automatic recalculation of ingredients (Urad dal, besan, Mathania chilli, Hing, oil, pouches).
  • Shortfall Detection: Live cross-referencing against Godown A stock; highlights ingredient deficits in red before authorizing runs.
  • Bay Allocation: Assigns batch runs to plant workshops (Milling & Kneading, Solar Polyhouse, Packaging).
How to Authorize a Production Batch:
  1. Open "Production & BOM".
  2. Select recipe on the left and choose batch multiplier (e.g. 2.0x for a 200 kg run).
  3. Verify raw material availability. Ensure no red "Shortfall" warnings appear.
  4. Click "Authorize Batch & Deduct Stock"; raw material inventory is reserved and the manufacturing order is issued.
Module 4: Multi-Godown Stock Management /v3/stock

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Dual Facility Oversight: Real-time inventory tracking across Savina Main Mandi (Godown A) and Sukher Processing Plant.
  • 3-Tier Stock Visibility: Physical Stock on floor, Reserved Stock (committed to orders), and Free Available Stock.
  • Reorder Point Alerts: Visual badge notifications when items fall below minimum safety levels.
  • Inter-Godown Transfers: Stock movement dialogue with source, destination, item, and quantity tracking.
Module 5: Customer Accounts & Sundry Debtors /v3/customers

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Customer Master Directory: Comprehensive list of retail stores, wholesale dealers, and HORECA partners.
  • Beat Categorization: Filtered by Udaipur trade beats (Savina Mandi, Hiran Magri, Krishi Mandi Hub, UIT Colony, Sukher).
  • High-Due Alerts: Color-coded indicators when a customer exceeds 80% of their credit limit.
  • Direct Order Creation: Click + Order on any customer row to open the order form pre-populated with that party.
  • Add Party Modal: Add new customers mapped directly to TallyPrime's Sundry Debtors group.
Module 6: Vendor Procurement & Inward GRN /v3/purchases

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Agricultural Supplier Directory: Regional mills and spice merchants (Mewar Dal Mills, Hathras Hing Syndicate, Mathania Chilli Mandi).
  • Purchase Orders (PO): Issue formal procurement POs specifying target godown and credit payment terms.
  • Goods Receipt Notes (GRN): Record incoming ingredient consignments; mark consignments as Received at Dock with physical weight intake logs.
Module 7: Payments, Cash Vaults & Aging Analysis /v3/payments

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Multi-Channel Payment Register: Records collections from COD (Van Vault), RTGS/NEFT (ICICI Bank Chetak Circle), UPI, and Cheques.
  • Ledger Aging Analysis: Visual breakdown into 0-7 Days (current cycle), 8-15 Days (driver follow-up), and 15+ Days (overdue).
  • Record Receipt Dialogue: Logs customer payment receipts and automatically pushes voucher entries to Tally.
Module 8: Statutory B2B GST Tax Invoicing /v3/invoices

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • CGST Rule 46 Compliant Format: Professional, clean B2B tax invoice with clear tax breakdowns.
  • Statutory Details: GSTIN 08AABCN9402M1Z4, FSSAI 12219034000412, HSN codes (19059040 for Papad, 21069099 for Sev).
  • Bank Remittance Details: ICICI Bank RTGS/NEFT details and payment terms printed directly on the invoice.
  • One-Click Sharing: Browser print formatted for clean A4 printing, plus direct WhatsApp sharing to customer phone numbers.
Module 9: TallyPrime Live 2-Way Sync Station /v3/sync

Key Capabilities Available Today:

  • Live ODBC Telemetry: Real-time connection status on port 9000 with heartbeat monitoring.
  • Bidirectional Sync: Pushes sales, receipts, and stock journals to Tally; pulls ledger balances and closing stock.
  • Voucher Audit Log: Live XML transaction history with voucher numbers, payload byte sizes, and timestamps.
  • Conflict Resolution: Tools to reconcile master data discrepancies (such as mismatched GSTINs).
  • Manual Full Sync Trigger: On-demand sync execution for instant Day Book reconciliation.
